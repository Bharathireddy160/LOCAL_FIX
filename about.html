WHY WE'RE HERE

Local connections
make life work better.

Finding someone trustworthy for a repair or everyday task can be harder than it should be. At the same time, talented local workers deserve a straightforward way to show what they do.

LocalFix is designed to bring both sides together through a clear, friendly experience — helping customers find services and helping workers get discovered.
